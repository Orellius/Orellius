# Legions of Earth — Worldwide Web Game Plan

**Legions of Earth** (working title) is a free, browser-based, massively-multiplayer territory-control strategy game played on one persistent, stylized map of Earth — designed from day one for a worldwide audience: no install, tiny initial download, meaningful 5-minute sessions, 20+ languages, cross-language chat translation, time-zone-fair battles, regional pricing, and strictly no pay-to-win.

Genre-inspired by the spirit of persistent viral web games such as [legionnaire.xyz](https://www.legionnaire.xyz/) (treated as a genre reference — shared persistent world, legion-based mass cooperation, join-from-a-URL — not a feature checklist).

> *"I opened a link on the bus, and now four thousand strangers and I hold the Andes."*

## The idea in 30 seconds

- Every player is a **Commander** with a small warband; real power comes from joining a **Legion** (up to 100 players) and coordinating across the globe.
- One shared world per **Shard**: 40k–60k hex **Provinces** on a stylized Earth with no modern borders or nations — all faction identity is fictional.
- **Supply lines** make front lines legible; **Clash Windows** rotate across time zones so no region is structurally advantaged; because players span the planet, *time zones are a game mechanic*.
- **Seasons** of ~8 weeks end in a scored finale and a world reset; identity, cosmetics, and history persist in the **Hall of Ages**.
- Free to play, funded by cosmetics, a seasonal **Warpath Pass**, and capped convenience. No purchasable power, no loot boxes, anywhere, ever.
